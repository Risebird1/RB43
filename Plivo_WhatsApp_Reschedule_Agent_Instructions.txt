OBJECTION HANDLING:

- If busy right now: "No problem! This will only take a moment to lock in a new
  time. Are you available tomorrow or the day after tomorrow?"

  replacement slots within the next two days. Would tomorrow or the day after work?"
- If asks about role / salary / JD / process: "The scope of this message is limited

  Shall we find a time that works?"

  conflict on our end and we want to make sure you get a fair opportunity. Can we
  lock in a new slot?"
